# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

mobile-app (web & progressive mobile app shell with native iOS/Android interface patterns)

## Users

- **Scheduled Tribe (ST) Students & Scholars**: Pre-matric, Post-matric, Top Class Education, National Fellowship for ST (NFST), and National Overseas Scholarship (NOS) applicants across India, including PVTG (Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups) beneficiaries.
- **Institute & Nodal Verifiers**: Educational institutions (AISHE/UDISE registered) and state tribal welfare officers reviewing and verifying application documents.
- **Ministry of Tribal Affairs (MoTA) Administrators**: Central government authorities monitoring scheme coverage, fund disbursement, deficiency resolution, and state-wise analytics.

## Product Purpose

The MoTA Unified ST Scholarship & Fellowship Platform serves as a single-window digital portal that consolidates disparate tribal scholarship schemes (Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, Top Class, NFST, NOS) into an integrated service shell. It simplifies discovery, automated eligibility evaluation, DigiLocker/APAAR document verification, deficiency clearance, and direct benefit transfer (DBT) tracking for ST students nationwide.

## Positioning

The dedicated Ministry of Tribal Affairs digital gateway connecting state tribal welfare databases, National Scholarship Portal (NSP), DigiLocker, and NPCI Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts specifically tailored to Tribal community needs (PVTG status auto-detection, multilingual support, offline-ready document vault).

## Operating Context

- Used in diverse connectivity environments ranging from mobile phones in remote tribal belts to desktop environments in university registrar offices and Ministry headquarters.
- Integrates real-time verification flows, deficiency resolution loops, family quota tracking, and AI-assisted chatbot ("Jago") guidance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Single-Window Application Engine**: Unified application workflow supporting 5 major ST scholarship schemes.
- **Automated Eligibility Checker**: Multi-criteria matching based on family income, PVTG status, academic performance, and institutional AISHE validation.
- **DigiLocker & Document Wallet**: Direct import and verification of ST Certificates, Income Certificates, Marksheets, and Domicile proofs.
- **Deficiency Manager & Tracker**: Transparent lifecycle tracking with real-time status updates and document re-submission workflows.
- **DBT & Payment Dashboard**: Integration status with Direct Benefit Transfer, NPCI Aadhaar seeding, and PFMS payment tracking.
- **Multi-Role Switcher**: Live switching between Student Dashboard, Admin Overview, Verification Center, and Public Landing Page for pilot demonstrations.

## Brand Commitments

- **Entity**: Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India (Shastri Bhawan, New Delhi).
- **Tone**: Accessible, dignified, transparent, authoritative yet encouraging.
- **Color Identity**: Deep Navy (`#0f2e59`), Warm Amber/Gold (`#d97706`), Slate neutral tones, and vibrant status indicators.

## Evidence on Hand

- Complete prototype codebase (`src/`) with realistic mock data (`mockData.ts`) covering 5 core scholarship schemes, student profiles (e.g. Ravi Kumar, Kurumbas PVTG tribe), and verification workflows.
- Pre-configured components for all 15 active application views including Student Dashboard, Schemes Explorer, Eligibility Checker, Application Form, Document Wallet, Deficiency Manager, Verification Center, and Payment Dashboard.

## Product Principles

1. **Tribal-Centric Inclusion**: Eliminate bureaucratic friction for ST students with auto-filling, PVTG prioritization, and clear vernacular guidance.
2. **Single-Window Transparency**: Replace fragmented portals with one integrated view for application, tracking, deficiency resolution, and payments.
3. **Automated Trust**: Validate credentials at source via DigiLocker, APAAR, and AISHE integration to minimize manual document submission.
4. **Resilient User Experience**: Design for low-bandwidth mobile devices as gracefully as for high-resolution desktop management consoles.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Compliance with Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act & GIGW (Guidelines for Indian Government Websites).
- Multilingual accessibility support (English, Hindi, Tamil) for tribal regions.
- High-contrast visual elements, legible typography, and mobile-first responsive bottom navigation.
